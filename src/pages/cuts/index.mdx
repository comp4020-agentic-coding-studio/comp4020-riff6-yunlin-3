# Commonplace Book of Cuts

The course asks every student to keep one of these from week one: a running
log of omissions noticed in the wild, each naming what was left out, what
it cost and what it bought. It seemed only fair that the site keep one
about itself.

This book is short, and it is meant to be. The assessment it borrows its
form from is marked on range and specificity rather than volume, and the
course's own rule against padding applies here first --- an entry for a cut
that was never actually made would be exactly the kind of gap dressed up as
a decision that week 12 exists to catch.
